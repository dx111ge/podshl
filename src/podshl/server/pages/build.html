Build your files

Fill in what your project can answer and what it needs to know, and this page writes .podshl/agent.yaml and one file per solution. Press Check with the mirror and the files are judged by the same code that ingests them — not a copy of the rules in this page — so what passes here is what the mirror takes.

Your draft stays in this browser. Checking sends the files to /validate, which reads them and keeps nothing: no copy, no log line. When they pass, put them under .podshl/ where your project is served, and prove control at /register.

Choose your agent.yaml and the solution files it lists, all at once. They are read by the same parser the mirror uses, and whatever this form has no field for is kept exactly as you wrote it.

Where it lives

Where .podshl/ will sit — the location you prove you control. The endpoint has to lie under it.

English is the one obligation. A reader in another language gets your words through their own model, marked as a translation.

What you can answer

Problem classes: one name for each kind of problem, like pip.install.wheel-missing. A client offers these to a person, so a solution for a class you do not list can never be reached.

The sentence is what the person actually reads. The name is for your rules; nobody outside your project can pick their own problem out of three identifiers. Write what they see — “Installing fails with ‘no matching distribution found’” — not what is wrong, because somebody who already knew that would not be asking. One line, 120 characters. Leave it empty and a client shows the name, which is all it used to have.

What to find out

Each is a fact your solutions can decide on. A reading is taken from the person's machine, with their consent, by the client — only operations from the published vocabulary, checked when your file arrives. A question is asked of the person; give it choices, or the answer never reaches you.

Solutions

One file each. It answers one problem class, when the facts you name have the values you give — and all of them must hold. The text below is what a person reads; write it in English.
