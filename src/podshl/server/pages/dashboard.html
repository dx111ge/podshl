How to read these

People is distinct people who reported, never occurrences, and almost nobody reports at all — so it is a floor, not a count. Nothing appears for a configuration below five people. An answer's total adds up its configurations, so somebody with two counts twice.

Worked / did not comes from what people said after trying your answer. An answer that helped nobody comes first, then one that worked in some configurations and not in others — usually two problems, and the row suggests the fact that tells them apart.

Measured was read from the machine. They typed was supplied by a person, and the difference decides what an outcome tells you: if your answer turned only on measurements, a failure is a defect in your rule. If it turned on something typed, the person may simply have answered wrong.

Your answer is found by walking your own solutions against these facts — the same walk /diagnose does. A suggestion is computed only from what this page shows, and nothing changes until you change your own solution file.

In their own words is text a person chose to send — usually the error, or the lines of a log around it. Names, addresses, tokens and times were replaced on their machine before they saw it, and it came only because they agreed to send it to you.

If this token leaked

Revoking stops every token for this domain, including the one you are using now — they are indistinguishable to you, because each was shown exactly once. Then prove control again at /register to get a new one. Your project stays published and stays mirrored; only the tokens stop.

If you no longer want to take part

Withdrawing stops the mirror and withdraws the attestation. Your anchor goes back to unknown — where every project starts, and which says nothing about anybody. Your tokens are revoked with it, because a live credential for an empty dashboard helps nobody.

Nothing of yours is touched. The files are in your repository; we only ever held a copy. Taking part was something you did, and stopping is too — you should not have to file a complaint against yourself or ask us.

It is recorded in the public log as self_withdrawn, kept distinct from a takedown on purpose: a project that left is not a project that was reported, and a log that could not tell those apart would make leaving look like an accusation. You can come back whenever you like.
